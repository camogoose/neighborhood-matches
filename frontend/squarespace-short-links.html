How it works

Start with a neighborhood, city, or town you already love. Then tell us where you’re headed. We’ll suggest three places with similar character—not simply the most famous tourist spots.

Choose up to three priorities if you want the matches to lean toward food, nightlife, arts, walkability, or another part of the experience.

About this place

This began while planning a trip to Denmark. The question wasn’t “What’s the Times Square of Denmark?” It was: “What’s the East Village of Denmark?”

That became the idea behind these destination dupes: use a place you know to find somewhere that may feel right in a place you don’t know yet.

Frequently asked questions

Can I enter a neighborhood, city, or town?

Yes. More specific starting places usually produce more specific matches.

Are these exact duplicates?

No. Places don’t have perfect twins. Think of the results as three thoughtful interpretations of what you love about your starting place.

Why might a result be just outside my destination?

A nearby place may occasionally appear when it is an especially strong match. A stricter search-boundary option is planned.

Why three results?

Each result emphasizes a slightly different part of the match, giving you useful alternatives instead of pretending there is one perfect answer.
